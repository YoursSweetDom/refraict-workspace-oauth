RefrAIct Workspace

A personal Google Workspace integration used by its owner with a local Workspace MCP server and the Antigravity desktop application.

Purpose

The owner can find, create, edit, move, import, export, download and move files to or from Trash in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides. The integration also supports owner-directed operations with Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Forms, Tasks, Chat and Apps Script, within the permissions granted by Google and the owner's account.

This is a personal integration, not an invitation to connect other people's Google accounts. It is not an official Google or Antigravity service.

Authorization and control

The owner signs in directly with Google and chooses the permissions. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings. This site does not collect sign-ins, OAuth tokens or Workspace files.
